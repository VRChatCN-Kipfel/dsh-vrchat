fix: manifest.presetPayload measured the suffix and called it the preset

The field summed t0-core, domain-map and the three t1 blocks — but those five ARE
the suffix: suffix = heading + t0 + always-load, and always-load = domain-map +
t1-world + t1-avatar + t1-shader. The prefix, the single largest part at 10,835
characters, was not in the list at all.

So a field named "preset payload" reported 2,717-3,948 tokens for what is really
7,250-10,800, and stats then labelled even that number "prefix + suffix". Two
layers of false precision on top of a missing term.

Replaced with presetInjection, which prices the two parts from the strings that are
actually injected and cannot drift back into block arithmetic. The order-640
section is recorded as null rather than folded in: it is assembled at runtime from
build provenance, so the build genuinely cannot know it, and pretending otherwise
is how the gap appeared in the first place. stats prices it at runtime.

The build report now prints each part and its characters, so a future mismatch
between "what we ship" and "what we claim" is visible at build time.
